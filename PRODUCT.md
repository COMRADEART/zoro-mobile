# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fitness enthusiasts who are also One Piece fans, training across three disciplines
(Mind / Body / Spirit). They open the app daily, often pre- or post-workout, on a
phone, frequently in low light. Their core job: log a training session, see whether
they're recovered enough to train, and feel progression (XP, ranks, rings, arcs).
The narrative theme is the hook; the tracking is the job.

## Product Purpose

Santoryu Fitness turns a personal training habit into a Zoro-themed progression
game. Success is a user who trains consistently because the app makes streaks,
recovery, and mastery legible and rewarding. The interface must serve the daily
log-and-check loop first; the anime theming is identity, not obstruction.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, focused, quietly intense. Three words: **honed, resolute, dark**.
It should feel like a swordsman's training log, not a flashy game HUD. Confidence
through restraint and precision, not through noise. Theme expression lives in
typography, kanji, and color, not in stacked decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon "gamer" fitness apps with glowing bars everywhere.
- Emoji-as-iconography (🔥👣🌙💀👑) used as the primary visual vocabulary.
- Identical stacked glass cards where every section carries equal visual weight.
- Sub-legible micro-typography (7-9pt uppercase labels) used to look "premium".
- The hero-metric template (giant number + tiny label + accent glow), repeated.

## Design Principles

1. **Legibility is non-negotiable.** No interactive or informational text below
   11pt. Hierarchy comes from scale and weight contrast, not from shrinking labels.
2. **Earn the card.** A surface change must signal a meaning change. Not every
   section is a card; group related content, vary weight, create rhythm.
3. **One icon system.** Kanji and a single line-icon vocabulary. No emoji as UI.
4. **Theme through color and type, not clutter.** The Zoro identity is expressed
   in the palette, kanji accents, and restraint, not glow lines and watermarks.
5. **The tool disappears into the task.** The daily log-and-check loop is the
   fastest, clearest path on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Body and label text >= 11pt, contrast >= 4.5:1 against the dark
surface (>= 3:1 for large text). Respect reduced-motion. Touch targets >= 44pt.
Dark theme is core to the product (low-light gym use), so contrast must be
verified against the actual dark surfaces, not assumed.
