Privacy Policy — Santoryu Fitness

Effective date: 2026-05-17 · Application: com.santoryu.fitness · Contact: allamsettiraviteja2009@gmail.com

Summary

Santoryu Fitness is an offline, single-player fitness tracking app. It does not have user accounts, does not connect to any server we operate, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. All of your data stays on your device.

What data the app uses

The app processes the following on your device only:

  • Fitness activity you enter — training sessions, exercises, reps/duration, mood, energy, sleep, recovery, and related progress.
  • Step count — read from your device's built-in step sensor via the Android Activity Recognition permission, to show daily step progress. Read locally, never transmitted.
  • App settings — theme, reminder times, and similar preferences.

This information is stored locally in the app's on-device storage. The developer does not receive, collect, transmit, sell, or share any of it.

Network

The app makes no network requests. It functions fully offline. There are no third-party services, analytics providers, ad networks, or crash-reporting tools embedded in the app.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push notification service is used and no token leaves your device.

Optional backup (Android Auto Backup)

If, and only if, you have enabled "Back up to Google Drive" in your Android system settings, the Android operating system may back up the app's local data to your own Google account / Google Drive. This is an Android feature controlled entirely by you. The developer does not operate, access, or receive data through this backup and cannot toggle it on your behalf. Turn it off any time in Android Settings → System → Backup.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Activity recognition — read device step count for the daily steps feature. On-device only.
  • Notifications — show local reminders you opt into.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback in the UI.
  • Run at startup — re-register scheduled local reminders after a device restart.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Data deletion

No data leaves your device, so deletion is fully in your control: use the in-app reset, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored app data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
