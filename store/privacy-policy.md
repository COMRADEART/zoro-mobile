## Summary

Santoryu Fitness is an offline, single-player fitness tracking app. **It does not have user accounts, does not connect to any server we operate, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.** All of your data stays on your device.

## What data the app uses

The app processes the following **on your device only**:

- **Fitness activity you enter** — training sessions, exercises, reps/duration, mood, energy, sleep, recovery, and related progress (XP, ranks, streaks).
- **Step count** — read from your device's built-in step sensor (pedometer) via the Android Activity Recognition permission, to show daily step progress. Steps are read locally and never transmitted.
- **App settings** — chosen theme, reminder times, and similar preferences.

This information is stored locally in the app's on-device storage. We (the developer) **do not receive, collect, transmit, sell, or share any of it.**

## Network

The app makes **no network requests**. It functions fully offline. There are no third-party services, analytics providers, ad networks, or crash-reporting tools embedded in the app.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules **local notifications** on your device (e.g., a daily training reminder). These are generated on-device. No push notification service is used and no notification token leaves your device.

## Optional backup (Android Auto Backup)

If — and only if — you have enabled "Back up to Google Drive" in your Android system settings, the Android operating system may back up the app's local data to **your own Google account / Google Drive**. This is a feature of Android itself, controlled entirely by you in your device settings. The developer:

- does not operate or have access to this backup,
- does not receive your data through it,
- cannot enable or disable it on your behalf.

You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → System → Backup. See Google's own documentation for how Android Auto Backup handles your data.

## Permissions and why they are used

- **Activity recognition** — to read the device step count for the daily steps feature. Processed on-device only.
- **Notifications** — to show local reminders you opt into.
- **Vibration** — for haptic feedback in the UI.
- **Run at startup** — to re-register your scheduled local reminders after the device restarts.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Data deletion

Because no data leaves your device, deleting your data is entirely in your control: use the in-app reset, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored app data from the device.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if a future version adds a network feature), this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version is released.
