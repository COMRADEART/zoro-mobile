# Content rating (IARC) — recommended answers

Play Console → App content → **Content rating**. The questionnaire is run by IARC and produces region ratings (ESRB, PEGI, etc.) automatically from your answers. Answer truthfully; these are the defensible answers for this app.

## Category

Select **"Reference, News, or Educational"** / app (NOT "Game"). Santoryu Fitness is a **fitness utility app**, not a game — it tracks workouts. The combat/sword theming is cosmetic naming and text only; there is no gameplay, no depicted violence, no characters fighting on screen.

> If the console forces a category, choose the closest **Utility / Health & Fitness / Productivity** path, not Game. This keeps the questionnaire on the app track (shorter, no game-violence branch).

## Honesty note on the One Piece theme

If you are unsure whether the *fantasy combat references* (swords, "boss", "defeat the boss this week") require disclosure: the questions ask about **depicted** content, not thematic naming. There is no rendered violence, so "No" is accurate. This is unrelated to the **intellectual-property** concern in `README.md` — content rating is about age-appropriateness, not licensing. The IP risk is separate and is the more serious one.
